Our Story

FROM PASSION TO PURPOSE

IronFit was founded in 2012 by fitness enthusiast and certified trainer Raj Malhotra with a single mission: to create a gym where every person – regardless of fitness level – feels welcome, motivated, and supported.


What started as a small 2,000 sq. ft studio with just 12 members has grown into a sprawling 25,000 sq. ft world-class facility serving over 5,000 active members. Our philosophy has never changed – results are built through consistency, community, and coaching.
